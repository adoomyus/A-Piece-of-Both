# A-Piece-of-Both
The advancements in Artificial Intelligence have led to extreme amounts of computational energy, while being constrained by thermodynamic limits such as Landauer’s principle. Quantum Machine Learning, shows a theoretical framework shift by using reversible transformations and high dimensional Hilbert spaces. This paper shows a Quantum Machine Learning framework by pulling quantum mechanics together with classical optimization techniques. This paper also shows single qubit geometry on the Bloch Sphere, quantum gates, complex structures of Hilbert spaces going from the inner products to Cauchy sequence completeness.  This work summarizes these mathematical ideas to illustrate how superposition and entanglement give advantages for the representation of complex data.
